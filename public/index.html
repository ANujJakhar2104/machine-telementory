About this Portal

The Machine Telemetry & Predictive Maintenance Portal collects live sensor readings from CNC and VMC machines, warns the team before a breakdown and turns every serious alert into a tracked maintenance ticket.

Developed under the Digital Initiative of SM Engineers as an internship project with the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, IIIT Nagpur.
